Thea is an AI-powered Discord bot that turns your existing documentation, wikis, and FAQs into an always-on support layer for your community. Every answer Thea gives cites the exact source passage it drew from, so members can verify what they read and your team can trust what went out. When Thea's confidence falls below the threshold you set, it escalates to your staff instead of guessing — keeping your community's trust intact. The same installation also runs layered automatic moderation, a private-channel ticket system with AI triage, and a publicly searchable help center, all powered by the same knowledge base.

## Why Thea

- **Citation-grounded answers** — every response links to the exact source passage, so members can read the original and your team can audit what was sent.
- **Confidence-gated escalation** — when Thea's confidence drops below your threshold, it hands off to your team rather than fabricating an answer.
- **Layered AutoMod** — four complementary layers (Discord-native rules, spam/scam-link heuristics, a moderation API, and an AI pass) work together to cover threats that any single layer would miss.
- **Scam and raid defense** — link canonicalization, QR-code scanning, identity/impersonation detection, cross-channel campaign detection, join-surge alerts, and an emoji-captcha gate protect your server around the clock.
- **Ticket triage** — incoming tickets are automatically categorized, scored for urgency, and analyzed for member sentiment before a human ever reads them.
- **Public help center** — the same knowledge base that powers Discord answers becomes a searchable help site, with support for a custom domain on paid plans.
- **Analytics** — track answer coverage, escalation rates, ticket response times, and moderation activity from a single dashboard.
