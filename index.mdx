Thea is an AI-powered Discord bot that keeps your community supported around the clock. It answers questions directly from the knowledge sources you connect — docs, wikis, FAQs — and always cites the exact passage it used. When Thea isn't confident, it hands the thread to your team instead of guessing. The same installation runs your AutoMod, ticket queue, and public help center.

## How Thea Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your knowledge sources">
    Point Thea at your documentation sites, wikis, sitemaps, FAQ pages, or Notion and Zendesk workspaces. Thea indexes everything in minutes — no retraining, no manual tagging.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tune your settings">
    Set the confidence threshold that controls when Thea answers vs. escalates, pick the escalation role your moderators hold, and write the rules your AutoMod should enforce.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rehearse in the Playground">
    Before going live, open the Playground in the dashboard to send real questions and inspect how Thea would respond — including the source passages it would cite and its confidence score. Adjust your threshold based on what you see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch in your server">
    Invite Thea to your Discord server. It starts answering questions in threads with citations, opening tickets from your panel, and escalating anything it isn't sure about to your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
