Reaching a monthly answer limit pauses new answers until the period resets — it never deletes your knowledge base or your conversation history. Every paid plan also carries a hard monthly spend cap as a circuit breaker.

Moderation is priced the same way. Discord's native rules, the spam and scam-link heuristics, the moderation API, the moderator commands and the escalation ladder run on every plan, free ones included. Only the AI pass that judges borderline messages against your own written rules is metered, because each scan costs a model call.
