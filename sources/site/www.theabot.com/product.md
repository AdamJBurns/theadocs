## From Setup to Launch in Three Simple Steps

Connect Your Sources01

Point Thea at your docs, wiki, sitemap, and past threads. It ingests and indexes everything in minutes — no retraining, no manual tagging.

Tune the Guardrails02

Set the confidence threshold, escalation role, and tone, write the rules your AutoMod should enforce — then rehearse real questions in the playground before anything posts.

Launch in Your Server03

Invite the bot and it starts answering in threads with citations, opening tickets from your panel, and escalating anything it is not sure about to your moderators.
