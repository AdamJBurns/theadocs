Thea's ticket system turns Discord into a structured support environment without requiring members to leave the server. When a member clicks the ticket panel button in any channel, Thea opens a private channel visible only to that member and your staff, immediately attempts to answer the question from your knowledge base, and then triages, categorizes, and routes the ticket to the right team — all before a human ever needs to type a word.

## How a Ticket Flows End-to-End

<Steps>
  <Step title="Member clicks the ticket panel button">
    A member visits the channel where you've published your ticket panel and clicks the button. They may be prompted to fill in a short open-time form (for example, an order number or platform) depending on how you've configured the category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A private channel is created">
    Thea instantly creates a private Discord channel that is visible only to the member who opened the ticket and the staff roles you've designated. No one else in the server can see or search the channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Thea attempts to answer from your knowledge base">
    Before any staff member is notified, Thea searches your connected knowledge sources and posts an answer with cited passages if its confidence is above your threshold. Members often get an immediate, accurate response at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Thea tags category, urgency, and sentiment">
    Thea automatically applies three triage labels to the ticket:
    - **Category** — matched to one of your configured categories (e.g., Billing, Technical, General)
    - **Urgency** — High, Medium, or Low based on the member's language and topic
    - **Sentiment** — Frustrated, Neutral, or Satisfied to help staff prioritize tone
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ticket is routed to the correct staff role">
    Based on the detected category, Thea notifies the staff role you've assigned to own that category. The right team sees the ticket without manual sorting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A human staff member takes over">
    The moment a staff member sends a message in the ticket channel, Thea steps back and stops auto-responding. The conversation continues between the member and your team with full context available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ticket is closed and a transcript is delivered">
    When staff clicks **Close**, Thea sends a transcript of the entire conversation to the member and prompts them for a satisfaction rating. Staff can download the transcript at any time from Dashboard → Tickets → History.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Claim and Assign" icon="hand-pointer">
    Any staff member can claim a ticket to signal ownership. Claimed tickets display the assignee's name in the channel header, preventing two agents from simultaneously working the same case. Owners can reassign to any other staff member.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Staff Notes" icon="note-sticky">
    Staff can attach private internal notes to a ticket — visible only to the team, never to the member. Notes persist after the ticket closes and are included in the staff-side transcript, providing full handover context for future reference.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Response-Time SLA" icon="clock">
    Set a response-time target per category (for example, 2 hours for Billing). If an escalated ticket goes unanswered beyond that window, Thea re-pings the routing role. Overdue tickets appear in Dashboard → Tickets → Overdue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Satisfaction Ratings" icon="star">
    When a ticket closes, Thea prompts the member for a satisfaction rating. Aggregated ratings appear in Dashboard → Tickets → Analytics and help you track support quality over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Canned Replies" icon="bolt">
    Staff can insert pre-written responses using the autocomplete picker or the `/reply` command. Canned replies are created and managed in Dashboard → Tickets → Saved Replies, keeping responses fast and consistent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reopen" icon="rotate-left">
    If a member replies to their transcript or requests a reopen, the ticket is restored with its full message history intact. No context is lost between sessions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Ticket transcripts are stored independently of the private Discord channel. Even if the channel is deleted after closing, the full conversation history remains accessible in Dashboard → Tickets → History.
</Note>
