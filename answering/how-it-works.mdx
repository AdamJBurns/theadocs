When a member asks a question in a channel Thea monitors, Thea searches the knowledge sources you have connected, scores how well the retrieved passages match the question, and posts a threaded reply with inline citations — all within seconds. If the confidence score falls below the threshold you have set, Thea escalates to your team instead of posting a speculative answer, passing along the full thread context and the passages it found.

## How an Answer Is Built

<Steps>
  <Step title="Member asks a question">
    A member sends a message in a monitored channel. Thea detects the question and begins a retrieval pass over your indexed knowledge sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Thea searches indexed passages">
    Thea breaks the question into search queries and retrieves the most relevant passages from your connected sources. Only passages indexed for your server are searched — no cross-server data is ever used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confidence is scored">
    Each candidate answer is assigned a confidence score between 0 and 1, reflecting how closely the retrieved passages match the question. The score is calculated before anything is posted to Discord.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply posted in thread with citations">
    When the confidence score meets or exceeds your threshold, Thea creates a thread on the original message and posts the answer with numbered inline citations linking back to the exact source passages (for example, **[1] Docs · Authentication**, **[2] Wiki · API keys**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Below threshold — escalation">
    When the score falls below your threshold, Thea does not post a public answer. Instead, it pings your escalation role in a private escalation channel, attaching the full thread, the cited passages it found, and the draft reply it would have posted — giving your team everything they need to respond quickly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Grounded by Design

Thea is built around a single principle: every answer must be traceable to something you chose to connect.

- **Exact source citations.** Each answer links to the specific passage Thea retrieved, not just the document. Members and staff can follow the citation and read the original text in context.
- **Server isolation.** Your indexed knowledge is completely isolated from every other server using Thea. A passage you add to your knowledge base will never appear in another server's answers.
- **Honest gaps.** When none of your sources cover a question well enough to meet your confidence threshold, Thea does not fill the gap with general knowledge. It tells the member it could not find a reliable answer and escalates to your team.

<Note>
  Thea never answers from general internet knowledge or its own training data. Every answer comes exclusively from the knowledge sources you have connected and indexed.
</Note>

## What Members See

Members receive a threaded reply directly beneath their question. The reply includes:

- The answer text written in clear prose.
- Numbered inline citations — for example, **[1] Docs · Authentication** — each linking to the exact passage Thea used.
- A short footer indicating the answer was generated by Thea.

If a second human (non-bot) reply appears in the same thread, Thea steps back and stops contributing to that conversation, letting your team take over without interference.
