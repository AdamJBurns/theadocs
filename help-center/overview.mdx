Your Thea help center publishes the exact knowledge you've already indexed for Discord as a searchable, public website. Members browsing the web and members asking questions inside Discord read from one shared knowledge base — when you update a source, both channels reflect the change automatically. There is no second site to build or maintain.

## What you get

Every help center includes:

- **Collections** — themed groups of articles (e.g., "Getting Started", "Billing", "Troubleshooting") that give your content structure
- **Articles** — individual pages sourced directly from your indexed knowledge
- **Full-text search** — visitors search across all published articles instantly
- **Your own domain** — point `help.yourdomain.com` (or any subdomain) at Thea on paid plans

| Plan | Help-center articles | Custom domain |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 10 | ✗ |
| Pro | 500 | ✓ |
| Business | 5,000 | ✓ |

## Enable your help center

Open **Dashboard → Help Center → Enable**. Thea immediately publishes your indexed knowledge to a Thea-hosted subdomain — no build step required. Share the URL with your community right away or configure a custom domain first.

## Organize content into collections

Create collections to group related articles together. Navigate to **Dashboard → Help Center → Collections**, click **New Collection**, and give it a name and optional description. Drag articles into a collection, or assign a collection when editing an article. Collections appear as top-level sections in the sidebar of your help center.

Good collection names follow the same logic your members use when they search — "Getting Started", "Billing & Plans", "Troubleshooting", "Account & Security" cover most communities well. Aim for four to six collections rather than one flat list of dozens of articles.

## Default URL

Your help center goes live at a Thea-hosted subdomain the moment you enable it. You'll find the exact URL in **Dashboard → Help Center → Domain Settings**. The Thea-hosted URL stays active even after you add a custom domain, so any existing links you've shared keep working.

## Custom domain

Custom domains are available on Pro and Business plans. See the [Custom Domain guide](/help-center/custom-domain) for the full DNS setup walkthrough.

<Tip>
  The help center and your Discord answers share one knowledge base. Update a Notion page or re-upload a doc, and both your Discord bot and your public help center reflect the change at the next sync — you never maintain two separate content sources.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Help-center articles count toward your plan's document limits. If you're approaching your limit, archive articles you no longer need or upgrade your plan before adding new content.
</Note>
