title: "Use a Custom Domain for Your Thea Help Center Site"

A custom domain lets your help center live at an address you own — like `help.yourdomain.com` — instead of the default address on theabot.com. A subdomain is the recommended setup: it is short, easy for members to remember, and leaves your root domain for your primary site. Thea handles SSL certificate provisioning and renewal automatically, so your team only needs to add two DNS records and check the status. Custom domains are available on Pro and Business plans.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro or Business
- Access to your domain's DNS settings (through your registrar or DNS provider, e.g., Cloudflare, Route 53, Namecheap)

## Set up your custom domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the help center settings">
    In the Thea dashboard, go to **Settings → Help center**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your subdomain">
    Type the full subdomain you want to use, for example `help.yourdomain.com`, and click **Connect**. Use a subdomain: most DNS providers cannot put a CNAME record on a root domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the DNS records">
    The **Custom domain** card shows two records to create with your DNS provider:

    | Type | Name | Value |
    |---|---|---|
    | **CNAME** | Your subdomain, for example `help.yourdomain.com` | The Thea host shown on the card |
    | **TXT** | `_thea-verify.` followed by your subdomain | The verification token shown on the card |

    Many DNS providers want only the part before your root domain in the **Name** field, for example `help` and `_thea-verify.help`. Save both records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the status">
    Return to **Settings → Help center** and click **Check status**. The card shows **Waiting for DNS**, **Verifying**, **Active**, or **Failed**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live with automatic HTTPS">
    The domain becomes **Active** only once its DNS actually points at Thea, not as soon as the TXT record is found. From then on, your help center is served at your custom domain with a valid SSL certificate, and article links and Thea's citations in Discord use it. Thea renews the certificate automatically before it expires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the default address behaves

Your help center always has a built-in address at `theabot.com/help/<your-center>`. Once your custom domain is **Active**, the built-in address permanently redirects to it.

If the custom domain stops resolving to Thea, or you click **Remove**, article links and citations go back to the built-in address.

## Who can claim a domain

A domain can belong to only one active help center. Connecting a domain does not reserve it: the first help center to verify it gets it. If another help center verified the domain first, **Check status** says so and the domain does not become active for you.

## DNS propagation

DNS changes can take up to **24–48 hours** to propagate across all global resolvers. Until the domain is **Active**, visitors keep using the built-in address, so no one gets a broken link while propagation completes.

If **Check status** still shows **Waiting for DNS** right after you add the records, wait 15–30 minutes and try again. If it still fails after several hours, check that the **Name** field does not repeat your root domain. Entering `help.yourdomain.com` where the provider expects `help` creates a record like `help.yourdomain.com.yourdomain.com`.

<Warning>
  Thea provisions and manages the SSL certificate for your custom domain automatically. Do not upload or configure your own SSL certificate — doing so will conflict with Thea's provisioning and may take your help center offline.
</Warning>
