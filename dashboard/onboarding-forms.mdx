Onboarding sits between join and full access. You can ask up to five questions, or skip questions and require a rules click. Answers are posted to a staff channel you choose. Thea does not keep them.

Configure this in **Settings → Onboarding**, or with `/onboarding` in Discord.

## The cards

| Card | What you set |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | **Ask new members to complete onboarding**, the panel channel, and **Post panel** |
| **Reminders** | Tag new members when they arrive, where to tag, and how often: Off / 12h / 24h / 48h / 72h. Reminders stop after **7 days**. |
| **The form** | **What the panel says** (up to 1,866 characters, because Thea adds a fixed safety line to every panel) and up to five questions (text, email, phone). Questions that ask for passwords, payment details, or identity documents are refused. |
| **Answers** | Staff-only answers channel. Optionally tell staff about members who never finish: Off / 6h / 24h / 72h / 7d |
| **Completion role** | Role granted on finish. Re-checked at grant time. Roles with moderator permissions, including **Mute Members**, **Deafen Members**, and **Move Members**, are refused. Choose **No role — just collect the answers** to clear a role Thea can no longer grant. |

If you move the panel to another channel, Thea posts it there and removes the old one.

You can only pick a completion role you could assign yourself in Discord: you need **Manage Roles**, and the role must sit below your own highest role. The server owner can pick any role.

You can run with **no questions** — the panel button is the whole flow (rules acknowledgment).

## Privacy

- Answers are posted to the staff channel only. Thea does not store, log, or train on them after delivery.
- If a question asks for email or phone, that staff channel must not be readable by `@everyone`. The dashboard refuses to save the form if it is. Thea checks again on every submission and stops collecting answers if the channel has since become public.
- Passwords, payment details, identity documents, and similar are blocked at save time.

<Warning>
  Asking members for passwords, 2FA codes, card numbers, or wallet phrases violates [Acceptable use](https://www.theabot.com/acceptable-use) and can suspend the workspace.
</Warning>

## Reminders

Reminders go to a channel you choose, not the panel channel. Thea batches members who have not finished rather than DMing each person. Each reminder tags up to 10 members; anyone else waiting is tagged in the next one. Completing the form removes them from later batches. Reminders run at most for 7 days after join.

## Raid mode and Discord screening

While [raid protection](/moderation/raid-protection) is active, onboarding reminders and new submissions pause so a join surge cannot flood the staff channel. The same is true while Discord's own membership screening is holding the member. Both resume when the hold lifts.

[Welcome](/community/welcome) is also held until a member passes the raid check.

## Discord commands

```
/onboarding setup
/onboarding disable
/onboarding questions add
/onboarding questions remove
/onboarding questions list
```

<Tip>
  If you only need proof someone read the rules, use a panel with no questions. It collects no data.
</Tip>
